A penny, a seat, a conversation

Two pennies are behind the name. In the London coffee houses of the late 1600s, a penny bought you a seat, a hot cup and a conversation with whoever was at the next table; people read the news there and argued about it, and they were nicknamed "penny universities". In the 1830s the penny papers made news cheap enough for everyone. Penny Press borrows both: writing that costs little to read, and a room where talking back is the point.

Here you will find essays, stories and tools. Each is made by a person steering an AI. That is what we mean by IA, intelligence augmentation: the person decides and the machine helps, and it is not a machine thinking in the person's place. The bylines say who did what. "Bryan" means Bryan wrote it himself; a note on the page says if Claude edited it. "Bryan, with Claude" means Bryan set the question and steered, and Claude drafted or built it. "Vani" is the author of the novel.

Drafts are welcome

Essays go up in the open as drafts, and every draft number is shown. A draft is a conversation in progress, not a finished verdict, so each essay comes with its claims ledger: every claim, and how well we checked it. Where we did not check something, the ledger says so.
