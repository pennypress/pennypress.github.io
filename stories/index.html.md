# Stories

Fiction made with an AI under a human author's direction. Free to read, free to remix.

- [What Do You Call a Tomato That Won't Sing?](../vani/index.html), Vani: A novel, free to read under CC BY-SA 4.0, drafted by an AI under a human author's direction. Chapters, a remix kit for your own stories, and a guide to trying this kind of writing yourself.
- [The Penny Universe](../penny-universe/index.html), Bryan, with Claude: A space opera in 37 dispatches about building automation, knowledge systems and AI agency, set aboard a ship that is also a coffeehouse. Walk it room by room. Written by an AI from real working sessions, edited for privacy, free under CC BY-SA 4.0.
