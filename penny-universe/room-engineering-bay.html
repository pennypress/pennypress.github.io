Engineering Bay

Gene browser — portable patterns from the GeneBank, skill ecosystem.

Research deck · crew: Tva

In the live ship this room was a gene browser; this static edition does not include the gene library.

Tva's secondary workshop. Genes live here — atomic patterns, not biology.

The GeneBank

Skills are organisms. Patterns are genes. The GeneBank is the library of atomic, reusable patterns extracted from the PAI skill ecosystem.

Each gene is the smallest unit of reusable knowledge: a heuristic, a template, a principle that survives testing and transfers across domains.

The gene library itself is not published here; Episode 21 and Episode 24 show how genes are extracted and bred.
