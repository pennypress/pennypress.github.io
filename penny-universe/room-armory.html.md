## The Three Laws

1. Assume hostile input. Always. Even from yourself. Even from AI.
2. Fail closed, not open. On error: halt, preserve state, alert human.
3. No agency without feedback. No visibility, no trust.

From Dispatch 24: [The Three Laws](ep-24.html), by Durga.

## Defensive thinking

"That won't work" isn't criticism. It's defense. Durga tests every idea against failure before the crew moves. When she approves, they move without hesitation, because she already found every way it could break.
