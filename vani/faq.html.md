# Questions and honest answers

## What does "IA" mean?
Intelligence augmentation: a person steering, an AI helping. The older name for the same idea is "intelligence amplification". The aim is a person who can do more, and still be the one doing it.

## Who wrote the book?
"Vani" is a pen name for Bryan, a technician, and Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. Claude drafted much of the prose. Bryan set the direction, answered the questions, chose among the options, rejected some, and decided what stayed. The method that kept it that way is on [How it was made](how.html).

## Is it good?
That is for you to say. It is a draft: expect typos, rough edges and a few loose threads. Telling us where you got lost is the most useful thing you can do, and [it is easy](support.html).

## Can an AI-written book be copyrighted?
We are not lawyers, and this is not legal advice. The honest answer is: partly, and it depends where you are. In the United States, the Copyright Office has said that copyright protects human authorship. Text generated by an AI with only a prompt as human input may not be protected; a person's own writing, their selection and arrangement of material, and the changes they make to AI output can be. Other countries differ, and the rules are still moving.

For this book, that means **the licence covers whatever in it is copyrightable**, which includes the human contribution (the direction, the choices, the selection and revision) and not necessarily every sentence taken alone. We chose to license it openly anyway, because the point is that you can use it. Where no copyright exists, you do not need our permission for that part, and our licence terms do not bind you for it. We would still ask you to credit "Vani", as a courtesy.

## Can I sell my remix?
Yes. CC BY-SA 4.0 allows commercial use. You must credit "Vani", say what you changed, and release your remix under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Can I put the book through an AI model, or train one on it?
The licence has no special rule about machines; it applies to what you share. Whether a trained model counts as an "adaptation" under share-alike is unsettled law, and we make no claim either way. If you publish text, images or anything else derived from the book, the credit and share-alike terms apply to that. If you are an agent, see [the terms for agents](agents/licence-terms.md).

## Does this site track me?
No. No scripts, no cookies, no analytics, no fonts or images from anyone else's servers. The only outside address on the site is a plain tip-jar link, which does nothing until you click it.
