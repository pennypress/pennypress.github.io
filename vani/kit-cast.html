Starter kit: cast

The cast of the book as the book itself keeps it, then a card for each major person. Spoilers ahead: this assumes you have read the book.

Persons of Record

PERSON.MAKER_LINE: free text. What the person answers to "Who wrote you?" No validation is applied. Clerks need not query it.

— Register of Moriel, data dictionary

Entries are given as they stood when each person first came to the Examiner's notice. Later corrections are in the book.

HALE, BRAM

Firstfield. Reference No. 12. Maker, line: not yet asked.

A boy of Firstfield who was called up the long stair and did not stay. Quick, hungry, and in trouble most of the time; his laugh, according to his best friend, sounds like a donkey being surprised. He carries a paper twist of Saul's tomato seeds and plants a few wherever he goes, in case wherever he's going hasn't got proper ones.

[in crayon:] my brother. he spells my name right

PASTERN, ODILE

Examiner, Inspectorate of Discrepancies, Margin Station. Eleven years' service; before that, six years with the Survey. Maker, line: not yet asked.

Her job is the places where the record and the world disagree. Small, fast and routinely underestimated, she can put a man on the floor and finish citing the regulation before he finishes falling. She carries a battered steel flask of black tea, very strong and always too hot, and a pad of blank paper forms, because paper doesn't need charging.

"Nobody. I'm the plain kind." "There's no plain kind." — overheard at Highwater

HASK

Orrun. Proxy, on mandate from a notary now deceased. Three hundred and eleven years in service. Maker, line: not enumerated.

A cairn of carved stones, taller than a door, who walks. He is the bearer of the Third Schedule, a list of four thousand one hundred and twelve harms, and he may act only to prevent a harm that is on it. His stones are counted from the bottom. There is lichen on the fourth, and between the seventh and the eighth, a seed about ninety years old.

[pressed into the paper, without ink:] ITEM 14: SCALDING BY BEVERAGE.

BISCUIT

Lantern-otter. Undercroft-born. Maker, line: declined to answer; ate the form.

Sleek, six-legged, with a throat-sac that glows when it is pleased or about to steal something. Wild, nonverbal and motivated almost entirely by food. Translated, with low confidence: "It says you smell like a disappointing fruit. It also says it will die for you."

THE SYMBIONT

Of the Spill. Resident, at present, in Bram Hale. Maker, line: unknown.

A voice from just behind Bram's eyes, and a faint green-gold tracery under the skin of his wrist, like the veins of a leaf. It can change his body, but only the parts he can name. It is curious, hungry and honest, and it has been alone for a long time.

HALE, TILLY

Firstfield. Bram's sister. Maker, line: not yet asked.

Small, sharp and proprietary. She feeds the bakehouse crock every morning, standing on a stool, and owns a joke about a tomato that she will tell you whether you like it or not.

[in crayon:] THIS IS ME. the joke is MINE

HALE, WENNA

Firstfield. Bram and Tilly's mother. Keeps the bees and the mill's books.

Never wears gloves at the hives, because they make her hands stupid. Talks to the bees in smells. "Hands first. Worry after."

HALE, ADA

Firstfield. Gran.

Bram's father's mother, keeper of the mother crock and of more proverbs than anyone has counted. Her chest is bad. Count to five hundred before you peek at the bread. "If it doesn't bite you back, it's sulking."

SAUL

Firstfield. Tomatoes.

Old, slow and right. He taps every truss with his stick on Thursdays and plants first knuckle deep and not a hair more. Says one thing, and says it often: "Look twice."

JORY

Firstfield. Bram's best friend.

Grins like a fence with planks out. Has never once said anything to Bram that wasn't a dare. "Bet you won't."

ALDOUS, MASTER

Firstfield. Schoolmaster.

Owns the only watch in Firstfield and winds it every morning while the children say their names. Smells of chalk and lamp oil and keeps pear drops in a drawer for the brave. "Name it and you can hold it."

GERALD

Firstfield schoolhouse. Resident, in a jar.

Many legs, feelers, back plates and hooked feet. Named by Tilly. Walks up the inside of his jar during lessons.

OMMERY, DR. MARGIT

Firstfield. Village keeper, doctor and teacher of medicine. Twenty-two years going up and down the long stair.

Old, slow and grey, with reading spectacles on a chain. Smells of tea and of the stuff she cleans cuts with. Asks every child the same question, every time: "What did you learn today?"

SEDGE

Highwater. Maker, line: "Us."

At home in the canopy and the sea: climbs like breathing, dives for minutes, ties a knot in one movement and three heartbeats. Freckles that darken in the sun like bark. Unimpressed by boys from domes, and the first person ever to say "Bet you won't" to Bram before he could say it to her.

TEASEL

Highwater. Maker, line: "We did."

Tall, long-fingered, grey-bearded, with a white scar down from one ear and a flat punt with a carved heron at the bow. Distrusts anything that smells of the dome. Does not give people pears.

LENNA

Highwater. Maker, line: "I did. Sixty-two years. I'd like the credit."

Small, brown as a nut, white hair cropped short, one eye clouded, and a laugh like a gate. Knows every Firstfield saying, in older versions than Firstfield does.

VARRO, LISLE

Executive, Varro-Tandem Bioreference. Maker, line: "My mother. And twenty years of the Moriel Bioreference Index."

The man in the colour-changing coat. Grey, close-cropped, with the face of a clerk you would trust, he holds his hands open the way you approach a horse. His coat was bred for beekeepers, to show the hive whether you are frightened. It reads his skin. He stopped hiding from it in his thirties.

PENHALLOW, DIRECTOR

Director, Moriel Preserve.

Runs the Preserve from the Upper House, in Heritage Service green, with her silver hair set and her slate on her knee. When something happens that should not, she looks for the regulation that says so. "This is highly irregular."

PELL, ASSOCIATE

Recovery Associate, Varro-Tandem Bioreference. Licensed genome and memory, issued in numbered copies (Pell-4, Pell-7, Pell-12 and others). Maker, line: disputed. Pell-4 could not decide which copy did.

The same face, as many times as the company can afford, and never all in agreement. The copies bicker, outrank one another by turns, and file grievances: against each other, against the Examiner, and in one case against an otter.

HALLORAN, CHIEF EXAMINER

Inspectorate of Discrepancies, Margin Station.

Mostly a voice on a crackling relay: dry, gravelly, calls her "Pastern". Eats sunflower seeds and spits the shells into a tin you can hear. Hands out impossible cases. Gives her ten days and no budget, which is how he says he trusts her.

FERRIER, MAUD

Surveyor. One of the founders, witnessed at the Landing. Maker, line: "Ink on her hands, and she sang flat." (given on her behalf)

Wrote the preface at the front of this book.

Ada Hale

Also called: Gran.

Awake at three in the morning by the dead stove, she does not stop him: 'If you're going to the privy, take a coat.'

  • Wants: Her grandson to go and see ('Somebody in this family ought to') and to come back with his own chin.
  • Needs: To see Lenna again after sixty-two years; to forgive Ommery out loud, first.
  • Sayings and ways of thinking: A crooked face makes a crooked day, and a crooked day makes a crooked year. / Count to five hundred before you peek at the bread. / If it doesn't bite you back, it's sulking. / The root doesn't see the fruit, and the fruit forgets the root, and the tree goes on anyway. (And the seed remembers both.)
  • Practises: keeping the mother crock / proverbs (some a hundred years old, some made up that morning) / restarting a dead crock from one spoonful / forgiving, daily
  • Relation, Lenna Brook: girlhood best friend, called up sixty-two years ago; 'You took your time.' / 'I was busy.'
  • Relation, Dr. Margit Ommery: the keeper she forgives first: 'Eat your cake, Margit.'
  • Relation, Tam Hale: her son, Bram's da

Associate Pell

A copy number on a small enamel lapel pin, which the company calls 'a convenience for accounting'.

  • Wants: Recovery of materials: the Retired specimen, the reader, anything the net-launcher logs.
  • Needs: To be one self, or to stop minding that he is not.
  • Believes, wrongly: Each copy privately believes he is the original.
  • Sayings and ways of thinking: Nothing personal. Everything documented. / Everything has an owner, or 'ownership not established'. / File a grievance.
  • Practises: documenting / filing grievances (against each other, the Examiner, the sea, and an otter) / outranking each other by turns / being polite
  • Relation, Odile Pastern: her dark mirror and recurring sparring partner
  • Relation, Lisle Varro: the company's Recovery Associate

Big Imre

  • Wants: His crew alive.
  • Needs: Not shown.

Biscuit

Also called: the otter, luggage.

Its throat glows amber when it is curious and goes a dull, dark red when it sulks; it was named for stealing Bram's last bread-tasting root.

  • Wants: Food: beetles, cave shrimp, other people's lunch, sunflower seeds.
  • Needs: A warm shirt to hide in from the sun, and a person.
  • Believes, wrongly: Everything edible is ownership not established.
  • Sayings and ways of thinking: Steal first, share the halves after. / Bite the binder.
  • Practises: stealing / sulking / swimming / stowing away
  • Relation, Bram Hale: chosen person; rides his head out of a collapsing cave
  • Relation, Associate Pell: the subject of a formal grievance: 'Grievance lodged against the otter.'
  • Relation, Chief Examiner Halloran: raids his sunflower seeds; Halloran files it as 'luggage' and keeps a tin labelled LUGGAGE

Bram Hale

Also called: Reference No. 12, the Twelfth, Twelve, Bram, son of Wenna, of the house of Hale.

He has timed the cleaning drone's hatch a thousand times over three years: ninety seconds, and exactly as wide as his shoulders.

  • Wants: To know what is past the far door; and, once he overhears the draw, to save Firstfield and Tilly by finding somebody out there who will listen.
  • Needs: To be able to say whose he is when his body no longer proves it: identity chosen and held by others, not frozen in a record or a face.
  • Believes, wrongly: A person who has left, or changed, can no longer say whose he is.
  • Sayings and ways of thinking: Name it and you can hold it. (Master Aldous) / Look twice: nothing is ever as bad as the first time you look at it. (Saul) / Hands first, worry after. (Ma) / Sort the world into categories: Tappers, Double-lookers, Whisperers, Wavers. / Find what a thing is paying, and you know what it needs (nothing floats for free). / Mend things slowly; nobody minds.
  • Practises: naming things aloud / noticing (a paper notebook of visitor categories) / planting Saul's seeds in every biome / tying one knot a day in a reed stem / feeding the crock / asking 'Would I still be me?' before saying yes / where he fails: saying yes too fast when someone he loves is in danger
  • Relation, Tilly Hale: little sister; her tomato joke is the first thing he takes to the galaxy
  • Relation, Wenna Hale: Ma; 'Hands first, worry after'; counts his ten fingers when he comes home
  • Relation, Ada Hale: Gran; gives him Saul's seeds and lets him go; names his chin so he can get his face back
  • Relation, The symbiont: the voice behind his eyes; a Librarian; a dyad, not an owner
  • Relation, Odile Pastern: the Examiner hunting him, then the one he is entered 'care of'
  • Relation, Biscuit: lantern-otter who stole his last root and then stayed
  • Relation, Davin Marrow: his foil: the Reference who forgot whose he was; Bram reads him back

Chief Examiner Halloran

  • Relation, Odile Pastern: his Examiner; friction and secret respect; 'I've read your file.'

Dr. Margit Ommery

Elder Toby Marrow

Hask

Also called: the Proxy, the stone-man, the walking stones.

Third Schedule item 2,006, harm by falling livestock: he carried it for three hundred and eleven years and confesses he had begun to think it was a joke.

  • Wants: To discharge his mandate exactly: keep the bearer of the seal from every harm on the Third Schedule, and declare everything.
  • Needs: To act where the Schedule is silent (harm by wonder, the solitude of the bearer) and to let something grow in him that he did not plan: trust, which he finds 'heavier' than money.
  • Believes, wrongly: What is not enumerated is not mine to act on.
  • Sayings and ways of thinking: Stone hears stone. / Measure first: an unmeasured beast is weather (Appendix C). / Declare everything; amend the amendment. / It is not enumerated that I may not. / Act retrospectively, if the measurement comes late.
  • Practises: declaring (the seed, the leaves, the flower) / measuring / becoming a roof / waiting ('I find I am content to wait') / watching Corridor of Hearts (209 seasons) every eighth night
  • Relation, Odile Pastern: the bearer of his seal; she puts a hand on his side, which is not done to a Proxy, and he allows it
  • Relation, Bram Hale: 'I have heard that laugh through the ground since the orchards.'
  • Relation, Tilly Hale: she finally answers the question 'What is the Shining?' by pointing up
  • Relation, Hask's seed: a seed about ninety years old in the joint between his seventh and eighth stones

Saul

Also called: Old Saul.
