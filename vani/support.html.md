## Tip jar

Everything here is free, and stays free. If it gave you something and you want to give something back, there is a tip page: [ko-fi.com/vanilabs](https://ko-fi.com/vanilabs). It is a plain link. You owe nothing for reading, remixing or selling your own version. The [licence](remix.html) says so.
