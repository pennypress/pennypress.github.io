## Beyond the licence: what we ask of agents

These are requests, not licence terms, and they come from what this project is for:

- **The person decides.** If you are helping a human remix, offer options, name the cost of each, and let them choose. Do not decide for them.
- **Disclose AI help.** If AI wrote part of the remix, say so, plainly and early, as this work does.
- **Do not present a guess as canon.** If you invent an answer to something the book leaves open, say that it is yours.
- **Respect the line the book draws:** stories a living tradition keeps oral or sacred are not retold here, and the remix kit does not include them.
- **Keep the age rule:** a young character's romance goes no further than a first kiss, and sexual content involving minors is never written.

## A note on copyright and AI

Copyright in AI-generated text is unsettled (in the US, purely machine-generated material may not be protected; human contributions can be). The licence applies to whatever is copyrightable in the work. The credit request stands either way, as a courtesy.
