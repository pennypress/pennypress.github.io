# Read the book

*What Do You Call a Tomato That Won't Sing?* by Vani. Book one of *What Was Sung at the Landing?*

This is a draft. Typos, rough edges and loose threads are expected. If you get lost, or something made you laugh, [tell us](support.html).

Much of this text was drafted by an AI (Claude) under a human author's direction. See [how it was made](how.html).

The chapters alternate between two points of view: Bram, a boy raised in a museum dome as a protected specimen, and Odile, an examiner whose job is the places where the record and the world disagree. You can start at the preface, or just at Chapter One.
