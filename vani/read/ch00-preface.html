These ponds keep the first copy of everything we brought, so that when it wears, as all copied things wear, it may be set right from the first copy and not from any living one.

Let the village remember the old ways by choosing them.

Let no child of ours ever be kept for a copy.

Maud Ferrier, surveyor, for the Library. Witnessed by all at the Landing.

— Preface to The Library of Moriel: A Survey of its Seven Ponds, with their Bearings from the Landing Stone
