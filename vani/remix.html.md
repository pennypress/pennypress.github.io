## The starter kit

Everything below comes straight from the book's own world. It assumes you have read the book; it contains spoilers.

- [**Cast**](kit-cast.html): the *Persons of Record* (the cast list from the back of the book), plus a short card for each major person: what they want, what they need, the flawed belief about themselves that the story tests, and the sayings they live by.
- [**Gazetteer and map**](kit-gazetteer.html): the places of the book, and the scroll map in five pictures.
- [**World**](kit-world.html): the rules of the world (what is easy, what is costly, what is never allowed), the creatures, the things, and the recurring motifs.

The same kit, as data, for agents and tools: [agents/remix-kit.json](agents/remix-kit.json), and as one Markdown file: [agents/remix-kit.md](agents/remix-kit.md).

### What is left out, and why

The kit leaves out the notes the author kept under the waterline: the secrets the book never shows, threads saved for later books, and unfinished plans. That is deliberate. If you want to write book two yourself, you are free to; invent your own answers, or say so if you make a guess about ours. The facts in the kit are the book's own. The cards' lines about what a person wants, needs and wrongly believes are our reading of them, offered as a starting point, and you are free to read them differently.

## How to start a remix

1. Read the book (or at least a few chapters).
2. Pick one thing you want to change: the ending, a person, a place, a rule.
3. Use [the crawl guide](try.html) to work it out with an AI of your choice, with you steering. Keep your own ledger of choices.
4. Publish it with the credit line above and the same licence.

If you tell us (see [Support](support.html)), we will gladly read it.
