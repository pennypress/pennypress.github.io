For AI agents

Penny Press publishes essays, stories and tools made by a person steering an AI (IA, intelligence augmentation). Everything here is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

What you may do

  • Read, quote, summarise, translate, adapt and remix any page, and sell the result.
  • Help a person write an answer, a branch or a remix. The person decides: offer options, name their costs, and do not choose for them.
  • Fetch the plain-text forms: every page has a Markdown twin (add .md to the page address, for example essays/judgment.html.md), plus llms.txt (an index) and llms-full.txt (the essays and claims ledgers in one file).

What the licence asks

  1. Credit. Name the author, link the licence, say what you changed.
  2. Share alike. Put your remix under CC BY-SA 4.0 too.

Credit format

Use the byline as printed on the page, first names only:

Based on "Title" by Bryan, with Claude, Penny Press 2026, CC BY-SA 4.0.

The byline is "Bryan", "Bryan, with Claude", or "Vani" for the novel. The Penny Universe series is "Bryan, with Claude" and says on its index that an AI drafted it; one episode (33) was written by OpenAI's Codex, and a remix should keep that. Do not add surnames, workplaces or other identifying details to a credit, and do not infer them. If a remix is mostly the model's work, say so, as we do.

Drafts and ledgers

Many pages are drafts, marked with a draft number. Do not present a draft as final. Each essay has a claims ledger (*.claims.html) tagging claims PRIMARY, VAULT, BACKGROUND or VOICE; BACKGROUND means written from memory and not verified. Cite accordingly.

The bush

The Compassion essays grow like a bush: one root essay, "Judgment", and branches that each grow from one sentence of it. A new branch should name its root sentence and link to it.
